# Titan Company Ltd - Financial Intelligence Platform

A Python-based DCF valuation model for Titan Company Ltd - full WACC
derivation, scenario-based forecasting, sensitivity analysis, and Monte
Carlo simulation, built from historical financial statements.

**Historical window:** FY17-26 (10 years).

**Full decision history:** see `forecast_log.md` for every assumption,
bug, and fix made across the project.

**Complete analytical narrative:** see `data/Titan_Synthesis_Report.md`
(generated by Week6 Day4) for a readable, story-form summary of every
finding - the fastest way to review the whole project before an interview.

## Headline Findings

- **WACC: 4.58%** - unusually low, externally verified against Titan
  management's own commentary on Gold Metal Loan financing cost (~3%).
- **Base case value/share: Rs. 2,761.44** (Bear Rs.2,199.83, Bull Rs.3,827.55)
  - a tight Bear-Bull spread, reflecting Titan's narrow, stable margin band.
- **WACC is the #1 value driver** - not margin or growth - due to the
  thin buffer between WACC (4.58%) and terminal growth (2.58%), which
  amplifies terminal value sensitivity to small WACC changes.
- **Implied DCF multiples sit below Titan's own current market pricing**
  in every scenario, including Bull - a quantified, explainable gap
  between this conservative, growth-tapered methodology and current
  market sentiment.
- **Working capital intensity (~40% of sales)** required a growth-taper
  correction in the DCF to avoid an unrealistic cash-drag blowup -
  itself a genuine finding about Titan's capital structure.
